No bot joins your call, and the AI is included in your plan, so there are no keys to set up.

## How it works

1. **Sign in.** Enter your email and the six digit code we send you. There is no password.
2. **Press record.** Start from the window or the menu bar. Your microphone and the meeting's sound are captured as two tracks, *Me* and *Others*, so the transcript always knows who said what.
3. **Press Summarize.** The audio is transcribed, your voice echoing back through the call is removed, and a structured report is written in the language you choose.
4. **Export, or ask.** Save the report as PDF, Word or Markdown. Forgot a detail? Ask AI answers from the full transcript.

## Plans

There is a **Free** plan with a small monthly allowance, and **Plus** and **Pro** with more hours of audio, reports and questions each month. Prices and what each plan includes are on [writewave.app](https://writewave.app).

## Privacy

Your meetings, transcripts and reports are plain files on your computer. When you summarize, the audio and text pass through our server to the AI providers we use, and are never stored by us. See the [Privacy Policy](https://writewave.app/en/privacy) for the details.

- You choose how long recordings are kept on your computer.
- Sign in is by email code. We keep only what an account needs.

Please tell people before you record them. Laws differ by country.

## First launch

The installers are not signed yet, so your system warns you the first time:

- **macOS:** open the app once, then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** in the SmartScreen dialog, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

After that, the app checks this page for new versions and updates itself.
